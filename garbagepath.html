Simple to Use, Powerful in Impact

1. Track Your Route

Press 'Start Tracking' and the app will draw your path on the map, showing exactly where you've cleaned.

2. Pin the Garbage

Find some litter? Tap 'Pin Picture' to take a photo and geotag its location on your route. Each pin is a victory!

3. Share Your Impact

Publish your completed route to the community map. Your efforts inspire others and contribute to a global heatmap of cleanup activity.

You Are Not Alone.

Every cleanup, big or small, is part of a larger story. The Community Map shows all the routes published by users like you from around the world. See the collective impact we're making together, and get inspired to clear a new path in your own neighborhood.

A Powerful Tool for Change

Public Profiles

Create a profile, write a bio, and earn achievement badges for your cleanup efforts. Let the community see the hero behind the cleanup!

Save Your Sessions

Keep a private log of all your cleanups. Save multiple named sessions to your account and track your personal history over time.

Export Your Data

Download your route and pin data as a GeoJSON file, perfect for analysis or use in other mapping and environmental software.

Custom Map Styles

Choose the map that fits your mood. Toggle between multiple map styles, including Streets, Outdoors, Satellite, and more.
