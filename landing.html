The Story

From Pavement to Platform

P1 Creations didn't start in a boardroom. It started on a daily walk. Navigating Chicago sidewalks, our founder saw a recurring problem: a city losing its shine to unmanaged litter.

Litter Bugs was born from a simple question: What if we treated the planet like a leaderboard?
