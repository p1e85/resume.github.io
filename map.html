About Garbage Path

The Mission (Why)

Garbage Path transforms the chore of cleaning up our neighborhoods into a visible and rewarding achievement. By tracking your cleanup routes and pinning the litter you collect, you create a tangible record of your positive impact. Share your efforts on the community map to inspire others, see our collective progress, and help identify areas that need our attention the most.

How to Use the App

  1. Join or Skip: Create an account with a public username to save your data to the cloud, or 'Skip' to use the app as a guest.
  2. Track a Route: Press 'Start Tracking' to draw your path on the map as you walk.
  3. Pin Garbage: When you find litter, press 'Pin Picture' to take a photo.
  4. Save/Load: Use the 'Data' button to save your current session or load previous ones.
  5. Publish: When logged in, 'Publish to Community' shares your route for everyone to see.
  6. Community View: Toggle 'Community View' to see routes published by other users!

Terms of Use
